## Defensible leadership themes

These are synthesis themes, not invented endorsements:

- **Hands-on credibility:** leadership roles coexist with shipped-product accounts and attributable code contributions.
- **Mentoring and hiring:** junior-hiring philosophy, curricula, technical training, one-on-ones, reviews.
- **Business alignment:** strategic-customer delivery, SOWs, staffing, migration goals.
- **Community stewardship:** organizing, speaker coordination, conduct/policy work, volunteer coordination.
- **Technical communication:** explainers, demos, interviews, conference sessions spanning backend, mobile, AI, and developer workflow.

For “strong leadership presence,” prefer these observable behaviors over an unsupported personality superlative.

## Before using community numbers

Ask for each number's date, platform, and definition. Slack membership, Discord membership, Meetup membership, unique people, active members, event attendance, and lifetime reach are different metrics. Do not add memberships across overlapping groups or claim all group events as personally organized.
