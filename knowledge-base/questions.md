# Questions to expand the career knowledge base

These questions focus on missing career context that could help with future resumes, not re-verifying information already in the knowledge base. Brief answers and concrete examples are enough; citations, exact timelines, and detailed metrics aren't needed. Outcomes can be qualitative.

## Projects and product work

1. **What work did you do for OpenAI, Wikimedia, and Primerica through This Dot?** What problems did you help solve, and what changed as a result? Anonymized descriptions are fine where needed.
2. **What are the most useful stories from your HUDL and Skylight work?** Focus on a major challenge, a decision you shaped, or a feature you helped deliver that isn't already described here.
3. **What was the biggest engineering challenge in vTestify's real-time transcription work, and how did you approach it?** How did it improve the experience for customers?
4. **What else about vTestify would help tell the story of your time as CTO?** Consider product strategy, enterprise customer needs, platform reliability, or scaling the business beyond the white-label work already captured.
5. **Which less-documented client projects are worth expanding?** For example: Duke, Adwerx, MAC Cosmetics, Vaco, Castra, BioConnect, Moogfest, Savings Seeker Rx, BWEL, or WeaveUp. For the strongest examples, describe what you worked on and why it mattered.

## AI engineering and developer workflows

6. **What are your strongest examples of helping a team adopt AI-assisted development?** What was difficult before, what did you introduce, and how did the team's work improve?
7. **Which coding-agent tools or workflow customizations have been most useful in practice?** What recurring problem did they solve for you or other developers?
8. **Are there other AI product integrations or consulting engagements missing from the knowledge base?** A short description of the use case and your contribution is enough.

## Engineering leadership and operations

9. **What are your best examples of helping engineers or teams grow?** Consider mentoring, hiring, developing leaders, or improving collaboration across your leadership roles.
10. **What important technical or business decisions have you led that aren't captured yet?** Examples might include choosing a product direction, navigating a difficult delivery, or aligning engineering with customer needs.
11. **What infrastructure, reliability, or delivery work should we know more about?** Focus on a meaningful system you ran or improved, a difficult incident you handled, or a process that made shipping easier.

## Community, teaching, and public communication

12. **Which community or teaching efforts have had the most meaningful impact?** Stories about helping people enter the industry, supporting speakers, building volunteer teams, or sustaining a community would be useful.
13. **What talks, workshops, articles, or advisory work are missing from the knowledge base?** The topic, audience, and why the work mattered are enough to start.

## Independent work and other gaps

14. **What is the story behind building and running Forecast?** What did you learn about creating, maintaining, and supporting a product used by other people?
15. **Are there open-source projects, maintainer responsibilities, or developer tools we're missing?** Focus on contributions you consider meaningful rather than an exhaustive inventory.
16. **What other accomplishments or hands-on skills are underrepresented here?** Include anything you'd want a future employer or client to understand that the current knowledge base doesn't convey.
